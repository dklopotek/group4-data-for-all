# Decision Statement

**This data product will help urban planning analysts and landscape architects at Barcelona Regional and Ajuntament de Barcelona (Espais Verds i Biodiversitat) decide where to allocate capital spending on barrier-reduction interventions (de-paving, urban cooling, new planting, mycorrhizally-compatible species selection) at each annual budget cycle by producing a ranked priority map of Superilla-scale zones (~400m × 400m) with per-zone intervention-type recommendations tied to documented Ajuntament budget lines.**

## Context

Barcelona is iterating on its green-infrastructure strategy through the Superilla (Superblock) and Eixos Verds programmes. Both include capital lines for de-paving, structural-soil intervention, new tree plantings, and urban cooling. Currently, these interventions are allocated through general planning criteria without a spatially explicit, data-driven priority ranking that accounts for the interaction between sealed surface, heat, canopy, and host-mycorrhizal compatibility. This product fills that gap: it identifies where each intervention type delivers highest leverage per euro of capital spend, using only directly measurable barriers the data can support.

The output is a barrier-reduction priority map — not a mycorrhizal network map, not a recovery prediction, not a belowground state assessment. It claims what the data can support: barrier concentration, intervention leverage, and budget-line alignment.
