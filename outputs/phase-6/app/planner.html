How to use this planner

Sequencing plane-tree (Platanus) removals to relieve the most pollen-allergen exposure, as a co-benefit of the city's biodiversity programme.

  1. Read sections top-down by priority. The red end of the ramp is highest priority (mature-plane source × residents nearby). Rank #1 is the single best place to start.
  2. Skip park-like / defer sections with few residents. Many high-source sections (e.g. Montjuïc) are parks — high pollen but almost nobody exposed. The budget planner can skip these automatically.
  3. Open a section by clicking it for its street worklist — which streets hold the planes and how many mature trees to take.
  4. Cross-reference the city works calendar to piggyback removals onto scheduled street works, paving, or tree-health interventions.
  5. Use the budget planner + Export CSV to turn a yearly mature-tree budget into a ranked, exportable removal sheet.
